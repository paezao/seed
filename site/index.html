How it grows

From a seed to your app,
one generation at a time.

  1. Plant

    seed new acme-ops gives you a folder with a kernel, a database and an empty app, in its own container.

  2. Ask

    Tell it what to become in chat, or point at part of the app. It plans first, and asks before guessing.

  3. Try

    It builds and tests the change in a sandbox, then lets you try it on a copy of your real data.

  4. Live

    Apply it: a new generation, a git commit, with your data backed up first. Roll back any time.

Under the hood

Careful by construction.

  • One container per Seed, with its own PostgreSQL, or an external one with DATABASE_URL.
  • Every change built and tested in a sandbox with a scratch database. Your live data is out of reach.
  • A kernel boundary: the app it becomes can't change the parts that keep it safe.
  • Git is the source of truth. Every generation is a commit; history is never rewritten.
  • Any model through OpenRouter, with your own key. Keys are passed at start, never stored.

Yours

Your app is yours.

Seed is open source under the MPL-2.0. The app your Seed becomes is yours, under the MIT license: take it anywhere. A Seed runs anywhere Docker does, from your laptop to a VPS or Kubernetes, with a volume and optionally an external database.
