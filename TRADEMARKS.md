# The Seed name and logo

Seed's code is open source under the Mozilla Public License 2.0, which covers
copyright, not names. This page says how you may use the name "Seed" and the
Seed logo (the sprout), so that people can tell the official project from
others.

**You may, without asking:**

- say that your software or service uses, runs or hosts Seed ("built with Seed",
  "hosting for Seed apps", "a Seed integration");
- use the name and logo to refer to the project in articles, talks and
  tutorials;
- keep the name and logo in unmodified copies, and in Seeds people plant.

**Please ask first before you:**

- name a fork, product or service "Seed" or something easily confused with it,
  or present a modified Seed as the official one;
- use the logo as your own product's logo, or in a way that suggests the Seed
  project endorses you.

A fork is welcome: give it its own name. Questions: paezao@gmail.com.
